---
name: Internal ledger boundaries
description: Cedarline balances and manual check review represent the internal ledger, not bank-held funds.
---

**Rule:** Admin-approved mobile check submissions post to Cedarline's internal ledger only. They do not clear checks or settle funds through a bank. Preserve existing balances as opening balances.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose manual Admin approval without a banking provider or settlement, and asked to keep the existing balances.

**How to apply:** Describe ledger updates as internal Cedarline records. Do not imply bank-held funds, cleared checks, or external payment execution.
